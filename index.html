Cabin Notes

Front Door

Upon departing please make sure key is in designated area and not in your pocket.

Oven Range

The knobs can be easily bumped and skewed off of the "Off" position. Please confirm they are all straight up and pointing to "Off" before leaving the cabin.

Toaster Oven

We prefer using the toaster over the range oven. Be cautious if there are coats hanging behind it.

Wood Stove

Wood stove should have some wood set up inside. Light it with a match which you can find in the top drawer in the cabinet to the right of the wood stove. Light the small white fire starter. Leave the door open approximately 1 1/2 inches for about 10 minutes and then latch the door. That will heat the home for several hours. If more wood is needed, there should be some on the deck. Please do not stock the fireplace with wood. Keep the fire size approximately the size of a shoebox.

Minisplit Overhead Heater Control

The minisplit heat (or air conditioning) source is above the couch. Use the control near the TV to turn heat on by pointing the remote at the indoor unit and pressing "Heat" (or "Cool" for air conditioning). Turn to the "Off" position when departing.

Back Bedroom Heater Control

Turn the lower dial to 1 to turn on the heater and back to 0 to turn it off. It may take 10 minutes to heat up.

Hallway Heater Control Next to Bathroom

Turn the dial to the marked position to turn on heater.

On a Septic Tank System

Please only put toilet paper in the toilet. Do not flush feminine products, wipes or any kind, or paper towels.
